PlayDrop is a pocket arcade: four quick games, daily streaks, levels and a weekly leaderboard. Play a round in a minute, collect coins, level up and see where you rank this week.

THE GAMES
🏗️ Skyline: stack a tower on a swinging crane. Time every drop and go higher.
✈️ Slipstream: dive through a canyon, thread the gates, don't touch the walls.
🔢 Merge 2048: the classic merge puzzle. Swipe, combine, reach 2048.
📋 Survey: six light questions about everyday life, no wrong answers.

COINS, LEVELS AND FRAMES
Every round gives you coins. Coins raise your level from Rookie to Player, Pro, Ace, Legend and Mythic, and every rank unlocks a new profile frame.

WEEKLY SEASONS
A new season starts every Monday. Every coin you collect that week counts toward your spot on the top-50 board. Finish in the top 3 and you wear the champion crown all next week.

DAILY STREAKS
Open the app every day to claim a daily bonus. Keep a 7-day streak and the bonus grows each day.

2X BOOST
Watch one short optional ad to double the coins from every round for 30 minutes.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Share your invite code. You get bonus coins from what your friends collect, and they get a welcome bonus after their first three rounds.

FAIR FOR EVERYONE
Scores are checked on our servers, with the same daily limits for every player. No sign-up and no email: tap Start and play. Delete your account and data any time from your profile.

Coins are game points only. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.
