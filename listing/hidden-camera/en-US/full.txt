HOW IT FINDS HIDDEN CAMERAS
• AI Lens Scan — point the camera at a smoke detector, clock, charger or teddy bear and the AI flags suspicious lenses, pinhole cameras and IR illuminators.

• Lens Reflection Finder — flashlight + camera glint scan, the same trick professional sweep teams use to find camera lenses.
• Magnetic Field Detector (EMF) — your phone's magnetometer finds electronics and magnets hidden inside walls, vents, mirrors and decor.
• WiFi Scanner — lists every device on the network and flags wireless IP cameras and unknown hardware.
• Bluetooth Scanner — finds AirTag-style trackers, GPS loggers, BLE bugs and unknown devices near you.
