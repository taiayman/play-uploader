Stuck on a scrambled cube? Point your camera at it and Cube Solver finds the solution in seconds. Then follow it one move at a time on a 3D cube, at your own speed, until every face is solved.

Works for the 3x3 magic cube (speed cube), the 2x2 pocket cube and the 4x4 cube. Whether you call it a rubik cube, a puzzle cube or a rubix cube, if it has six colors, this cube solver can solve it.

📷 SCAN WITH THE CAMERA
• Hold each face in front of the camera. Every sticker shows its detected color live
• Auto-capture as soon as you hold the cube steady, or tap to capture
• Works with any color scheme and in normal room light
• Tap any sticker to fix it, or enter all colors by hand if you prefer
• The scanner checks the cube before solving and tells you exactly what to look at if a color looks wrong

🧊 SOLUTION STEP BY STEP IN 3D
• A 3D cube shows every move, with an arrow on the face you have to turn
• Next, Back and Play, at 0.5×, 1× or 2× speed
• Each move is written out in plain words, like "Right face clockwise", next to the notation
• 3x3 cube solver: about 20 moves
• 2x2 cube solver: the shortest possible solution
• 4x4 cube solver: full solve with parity included, step by step

🎓 LEARN HOW TO SOLVE IT YOURSELF: GUIDE AND TUTORIAL
• Beginner method in 7 steps: white cross, white corners, middle layer, yellow cross, yellow edges, place and twist the corners
• Each step shows the goal and the algorithm, animated on a 3D cube
• Cube notation explained with moving cubes: R, U', F2, and 4x4 moves like Rw and r
• Easy for complete beginners. No speedcubing background needed

⏱ SPEEDCUBE TIMER
• Hold to start, release to go: a real speedcubing timer
• Random scrambles for 3x3, 2x2 and 4x4 with a preview of the scrambled cube
• Best time, Ao5 and Ao12, +2 and DNF, full history of your solves

✔ ALSO
• Works offline: no wifi or internet needed to solve
• No account, no sign-up
• Light and dark mode

Who is it for? Anyone who got a cube as a gift and can't get it back, beginners who want to learn the layer-by-layer method, and speedcubers who want a clean cube timer with real scrambles.

HOW TO SOLVE A CUBE WITH THE APP
1. Pick 3x3, 2x2 or 4x4
2. Scan the six faces with the camera, in the order shown on screen
3. Check the colors, then tap Solve
4. Hold the cube like the 3D cube on screen and follow the arrows

QUESTIONS
Does it work with a 2x2 pocket cube or a 4x4 cube? Yes, both, with the camera or by hand.
Do I need internet? No. The cube solver works offline.
My stickers are not the standard colors. Any six colors work, the app groups them for you.
Can I learn instead of just copying moves? Yes, the Learn tab teaches the beginner method step by step.
